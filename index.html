About ERDSE

Various systems and services, including generative AI, are transforming our society through the use of massive amounts of data. Their development requires advances in both data science and data engineering, as well as careful consideration of privacy, security, and fairness.

ERDSE aims to provide a forum for presenting and discussing emerging research at the intersection of data science and data engineering, particularly encouraging participation from young researchers and students.

Topics of Interest

  • Data Science and AI: Large Language Models (LLM), Explainable AI (XAI), Interpretable Machine Learning
  • Data Engineering and Databases: Big Data, Graph Data, Data Security, Data Privacy
  • Information Retrieval and Recommendation: Social Media, Information Credibility, Polarization and Echo Chambers
  • Multimedia Data: Media Understanding, AI Generated Content, Multimodal AI
  • Human Factors: Human Computation, Crowdsourcing, Human-in-the-Loop Methods, Bias and Fairness
  • Application Domains: Healthcare, Education, Mobility
